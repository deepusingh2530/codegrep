# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in codegrep to be a good experience for everyone,
regardless of experience level, background, or seniority. Harassment,
discrimination, and personal attacks are not tolerated in any project space:
issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, or commit messages.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith. Disagree with the code, not the person.
- Be specific and technical. "This allocates per match inside the hot loop"
  beats "this is bad".
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change, and ask why rather than
  relitigating in the thread.
- Credit others when you build on their work.
- Keep it professional. No slur or sarcasm aimed at people.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, or intimidation of any kind.
- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or spam.

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
commits) and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer privately — see
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for how to reach us without opening a public issue
first. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers will review every
report, and the outcome may include a private correction, a public warning, or
a ban from project spaces.

Maintainers are expected to follow this policy themselves. If a maintainer is
the subject of a report, it goes to the platform rather than to that
maintainer.

This policy is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
